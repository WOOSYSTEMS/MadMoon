## YOUR QUERY LETTER

Use this as your template. Personalize the first line for each agent.

---

Dear [Agent Name],

I am seeking representation for UNIDENTIFIED SPEAKER, a literary novel of 43,000 words.

On a Thursday in May 2041, the CEO of a biotech company sits down before a congressional subcommittee and asks to testify about a pediatric drug nobody has questioned. Over five hours, under oath, answering every question completely, he tells a room full of legislators that something is coming through the air that will kill every adult on Earth — and that the only thing he could give anyone was two days' warning and a box for their children.

The novel follows the hearing in real time and then follows the two days: a truck driver in Maryland who finishes his route because the manifest says to. A charge nurse who allocates pods she will never use. A hardware store owner who opens on the last morning because that is what he does at seven. A congressional staffer who sits in a hallway with her hand on a sealed box containing her son, telling him about a trowel she bought for no reason. And a network that watches nine billion people spend their final hours being, by almost every measure, better than it predicted.

UNIDENTIFIED SPEAKER will appeal to readers of Kazuo Ishiguro's Never Let Me Go and Cormac McCarthy's The Road — novels that use speculative premises to examine what endures when everything else is taken away.

This is my debut novel. I am a Sri Lankan-born writer based in British Columbia, Canada.

Thank you for your time and consideration. This is a simultaneous submission. The complete manuscript is available upon request.

Nuwan Ambagahawatte
[your email]

## WORD COUNT NOTE

43K is short for a debut novel (industry standard is 70-100K). Here's how to handle it:

- Call it "a novel" — never "a novella"
- Put the word count at the end of the first paragraph, not the opening line
- Let your comp titles do the work: The Road (58K), Klara and the Sun (58K), On Chesil Beach (40K)
- If an agent asks, say: "The structure is complete. The novel is the length it needs to be."

## RULES

1. Simultaneous submissions are standard. Query as many agents as you want.
2. If an agent offers representation, email ALL other agents who have your query: "I have received an offer of representation and wanted to let you know before I respond. I would be happy to send the full manuscript if you are interested."
3. Never pay an agent upfront. Agents earn 15% commission on sales. If they ask for money before selling your book, they are not a real agent.
4. Response times: 2-12 weeks for queries, 6-16 weeks for full manuscripts.
5. No response after double the stated time = pass. Move on.
