## PHASE 1: GO LEGAL (Nov 2026)

**Total cost: ~$632-$812**

| Step | Cost | Timeline |
|---|---|---|
| BC Sole Proprietorship registration | $70 | Same day online |
| Kamloops home-based business license | ~$92 | 1-2 weeks |
| Product liability insurance (Indie Business Network) | ~$400/yr | 1 week |
| Health Canada Cosmetic Notification Form | FREE | Within 10 days of first sale |
| Proper labels printed (bilingual EN/FR) | ~$50-100 | 1 week |
| Cosmetic-grade tins (30g) — first batch of 20 | ~$20-50 | AliExpress/Amazon |

**Label requirements (Canadian law):**
```
BadMoon — Solid Cologne / Cologne Solide
Net wt / Poids net: 30 g (1 oz)

Ingredients: Petrolatum, Cedrus Atlantica Bark Oil,
Mentha Viridis Leaf Oil

Made by / Fabriqué par:
Nuwan Ambagahawatte
Kamloops, BC, Canada

Patch test before use / Tester avant utilisation
```

**INCI Names:**
- Vaseline → Petrolatum
- Cedarwood oil → Cedrus Atlantica Bark Oil
- Spearmint oil → Mentha Viridis Leaf Oil

**Safety limits:**
- Spearmint oil: max 1.7% dermal (Tisserand)
- Cedarwood oil: max 4-5% dermal
- Current formula: ~2% total — SAFE

**Claims you CAN make:** Smells great, long-lasting scent, natural ingredients, handcrafted
**Claims you CANNOT make:** Heals, treats, cures, anti-inflammatory, antibacterial, therapeutic

## THE ONEBRAIN CONNECTION

BadMoon is not just cologne. It's applied sensory influence research.

| OneBrain Division | Sense | Method | Application |
|---|---|---|---|
| Frequency Engine | Sound | Brainwave entrainment, sub-threshold audio | Focus, sleep, behavioral influence |
| BadMoon | Smell | Scent psychology, olfactory influence | Trust, attraction, authority, spending |

**Key research findings applied:**
- Lavender increases trust (Leiden University, p<0.05)
- Cedar conveys reliability and competence
- Simple scents outperform complex ones for behavioral influence (Washington State, N=400+)
- Scent bypasses conscious thought → limbic system → decision-making
- Same neural pathway as sub-threshold frequencies

**The boardroom play:** Wear BadMoon's trust blend to a meeting. The person across the table subconsciously trusts you more. Same thesis as ambient frequency design — influence through the senses, below conscious awareness.
