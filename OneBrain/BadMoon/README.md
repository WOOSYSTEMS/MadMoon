## RECIPE

**Base:** Vaseline Original petroleum jelly (100 g jar)
**Scent:** Buhbli cedarwood essential oil (30 ml) + Buhbli spearmint essential oil (30 ml)

**Per 100 g jar:**
- 30 drops cedarwood
- 10 drops spearmint
- ~2% dilution (skin safe)

**Method:**
1. Mix drops directly into the Vaseline jar
2. Stir 1–2 minutes
3. Rest 24–48 hours for best scent infusion

**Usage:** Pea-sized amount on wrists, neck, behind ears. One jar lasts ~7–9 months at once daily.

**Yield:** Each 30 ml oil bottle can scent ~15 jars.

---

## SAFETY

- Patch test before first use
- Never apply undiluted essential oil to skin
- Keep mint away from face/eyes
- Rejected "Vanilla Bean" home fragrance oil — not skin-safe
- This replaces fragrance, NOT underarm deodorant

## LEGAL CHECKLIST (Selling in Canada)

- [ ] **Health Canada Cosmetic Notification Form** — must file within 10 days of first sale (not needed for free samples)
- [ ] **Ingredient labelling** — INCI names required on packaging (Petrolatum, Cedrus Atlantica Bark Oil, Mentha Spicata Leaf Oil)
- [ ] **Bilingual labelling** — English + French required for retail sale in Canada
- [ ] **Net quantity** — weight must be stated on label
- [ ] **Manufacturer name & address** — must appear on label
- [ ] **No therapeutic claims** — cannot claim it heals, treats, or prevents anything (that makes it a drug, not a cosmetic)
- [ ] **Proper packaging** — food-grade or cosmetic-grade containers (pill boxes OK for samples, not for sale)
- [ ] **Liability consideration** — consider basic product liability insurance if selling beyond coworkers
- [ ] **Business registration** — if revenue exceeds $30K/yr, need GST/HST registration

*Free samples require none of the above. Only triggers when you sell.*
